### Description of scenarios for using the module

The Consent extension module provides the electronic representation of the [MII Consent](https://www.medizininformatik-initiative.de/de/mustertext-zur-patienteneinwilligung) and can, beyond that, also be used for other consent documents.

One important measure for medical research is, among others, how many patients meet particular criteria (case numbers) and whether those patients have consented to the use of their data for research purposes.

Representing consent information in the core dataset in a standardized way is required so that it can be taken into account as a search criterion, in particular for cross-site enquiries.

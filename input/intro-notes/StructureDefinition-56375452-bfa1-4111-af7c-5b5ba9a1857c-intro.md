Building on the
[recommendations](https://ig.fhir.de/einwilligungsmanagement/stable/DocumentReference.html)
of the AG Einwilligungsmanagement, the profile
*MII\_PR\_Consent\_DocumentReference* targets the representation of scanned
consent documents — restricted to scans of consent documents in PDF format.
